
# 🌱 SM CleanTech Engineering Solutions

## Industrial B2B CleanTech Networking & Vendor Matching Platform

SM CleanTech Engineering Solutions is a B2B platform designed to connect industrial buyers with relevant CleanTech, engineering, EPC, environmental and compliance vendors.

The platform allows buyers to submit structured technical requirements and automatically matches them with verified vendors based on their registered CleanTech domains.

The system supports:

- Buyer Registration & Login
- Vendor Registration & Login
- Email OTP Verification
- Role-Based Authentication
- Technical Enquiry Creation
- Automatic Enquiry Approval
- Domain-Based Vendor Matching
- Technical Questionnaire
- Vendor Matching Suggestions
- Technical Dossier
- Vendor Quotations
- Buyer Quotation Acceptance
- Vendor Acceptance
- Two-Way Handshake
- Contact Detail Unlock
- CRM History
- Email Notifications
- Admin Dashboard
- Vendor & Buyer Management

# 🚀 Project Overview

SM CleanTech is an Industrial B2B CleanTech marketplace and technical enquiry platform.

The main purpose is to connect:

**Industrial Buyers → Technical Requirements → Relevant Vendors → Quotations → Mutual Handshake**

Instead of buyers searching for vendors manually, the platform uses the buyer's selected CleanTech domain and problem/category to identify relevant registered vendors.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Industrial companies often need specialized solutions related to:

- Water Treatment
- Waste Management
- Renewable Energy
- Carbon Management
- ESG
- Air Pollution
- Environmental Compliance
- SPCB Approvals
- Engineering Solutions

Finding the right technical vendor manually can be time-consuming.

The platform solves this problem by providing a structured digital workflow.

---

# 💡 Solution

The platform provides a controlled B2B workflow:

```text
Buyer Registration
       ↓
Email OTP Verification
       ↓
Buyer Login
       ↓
Create Technical Enquiry
       ↓
Select CleanTech Domain
       ↓
Select Problem / Sub-Domain
       ↓
Answer Technical Questions
       ↓
Submit Enquiry
       ↓
Enquiry Automatically Approved
       ↓
Domain-Based Vendor Matching
       ↓
Matched Vendors Receive Enquiry
       ↓
Vendor Reviews Technical Dossier
       ↓
Vendor Submits Quotation
       ↓
Buyer Reviews Quotation
       ↓
Buyer Accepts Quotation
       ↓
Vendor Accepts Connection
       ↓
Two-Way Handshake
       ↓
Contact Details Unlocked
