1. COMPANY / PLATFORM OVERVIEW
============================================================

Company / platform name
-----------------------
SM CleanTech Engineering Solutions

Primary positioning
-------------------
SM CleanTech Engineering Solutions is an industrial B2B CleanTech network and
technical matching platform.

The platform is designed to connect genuine industrial requirements with relevant
engineering, EPC, and CleanTech solution providers through a structured and
transparent technical matching workflow.

Website positioning statement
-----------------------------
"Diagnose. Match. Solution."

Core idea
---------
Industrial businesses may have technical, environmental, sustainability, energy,
waste, water, pollution-control, or compliance-related requirements. The platform
is designed to structure those requirements and connect them with relevant
technical solution providers.

The platform has two major business-side participant types:
- Buyer
- Vendor

Buyer
-----
A Buyer represents an industrial business or organization that has a requirement,
project, problem, technical need, or CleanTech-related enquiry.

A buyer can register on the platform, provide company information, create an
industrial enquiry, answer relevant technical questions, and participate in the
quotation and mutual-handshake workflow.

Vendor
------
A Vendor represents an engineering, EPC, CleanTech, or technical solution provider
that can potentially respond to relevant industrial requirements.

A vendor can register, provide company and capability information, identify its
industry type, area of work, experience, capacity, specialization, GST information,
website, and other profile information, and participate in relevant opportunities.

Admin
-----
The platform also has an Admin role for platform operations, management, and
workflow administration.

The Admin role is separate from Buyer and Vendor accounts.

The website should not tell a visitor that a particular Admin action has happened
unless the backend record confirms it.

4.1 WATER & WASTEWATER
------------------------------------------------------------

Water & Wastewater is one of the platform's core solution areas.

Friendly explanation:

ETP
---
ETP stands for Effluent Treatment Plant.

If asked what ETP means:
"ETP means Effluent Treatment Plant. It is generally associated with treatment
of industrial effluent. For project-specific technical advice, the requirement
should be reviewed by a qualified technical provider."

STP
---
STP stands for Sewage Treatment Plant.

ZLD
---
ZLD stands for Zero Liquid Discharge.

CETP
----
CETP stands for Common Effluent Treatment Plant.

MEE
---
MEE stands for Multiple Effect Evaporator.

MBR
---
MBR stands for Membrane Bioreactor.

MBBR
----
MBBR stands for Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor.

RO
--
RO stands for Reverse Osmosis.

UF
--
UF stands for Ultrafiltration.

MF
--
MF stands for Microfiltration.

MVR
---
MVR stands for Mechanical Vapor Recompression.

ASP
---
ASP stands for Activated Sludge Process.

SBR
---
SBR stands for Sequencing Batch Reactor.

DM / Ion Exchange
-----------------
DM commonly refers to Demineralization, and ion exchange is a water-treatment
process family. Exact process selection depends on the project requirement.

Crystallizer
------------
A crystallizer can be part of a treatment system where crystallization is required.
The platform should not provide a project-specific design without a qualified
technical assessment.

============================================================
12. QUOTATION WORKFLOW
============================================================

A quotation is a vendor response to a buyer opportunity.

General flow:

1. Buyer creates an enquiry.
2. The platform identifies relevant opportunities.
3. A vendor reviews an opportunity.
4. Vendor submits a quotation.
5. Buyer reviews the quotation.
6. Buyer can accept or reject according to the platform workflow.
7. Vendor can accept or reject / proceed according to the workflow.
8. A mutual handshake can occur when both sides accept.
9. Contact details can then be unlocked according to the platform rules.

Important:
The chatbot should never invent a quotation amount.

If user asks:
"How much will the vendor quote?"

Answer:
"Quotation values are provided by the relevant vendor based on the project
requirement. I don't have a fixed price list for vendor quotations."

13. MUTUAL HANDSHAKE
============================================================

The mutual handshake is an important platform concept.

Purpose:
To provide a controlled way for buyer and vendor parties to mutually accept the
connection before contact details are unlocked.

Concept:

Buyer accepts
     +
Vendor accepts
     =
Mutual handshake

After the relevant mutual acceptance:
Contact details can be unlocked according to the platform workflow.

Important:
A mutual handshake is not a guarantee of:
- project award
- contract
- payment
- project completion
- technical acceptance

It represents the platform's connection / contact-unlock step.

29. PLATFORM WORKFLOW - DETAILED EXPLANATION
============================================================

STEP 1 - BUYER REGISTRATION
--------------------------
The industrial organization creates a Buyer account and provides the requested
company and contact information.

STEP 2 - CREATE ENQUIRY
-----------------------
The Buyer describes the industrial requirement through the platform.

STEP 3 - SELECT DOMAIN / PROBLEM
---------------------------------
The Buyer can select the relevant CleanTech domain and problem category.

STEP 4 - TECHNICAL QUESTIONS
----------------------------
The platform can present structured technical questions relevant to the selected
problem.

STEP 5 - SUBMIT REQUIREMENT
---------------------------
The Buyer submits the structured requirement.

STEP 6 - MATCHING
-----------------
The platform uses the available requirement and business information to identify
relevant solution-provider opportunities.

STEP 7 - VENDOR OPPORTUNITY
---------------------------
A relevant Vendor can review the available opportunity information.

STEP 8 - QUOTATION
------------------
The Vendor can submit a quotation / response through the platform workflow.

STEP 9 - BUYER REVIEW
---------------------
The Buyer reviews the quotation / response.

STEP 10 - ACCEPTANCE
--------------------
The Buyer and Vendor can proceed through the relevant acceptance workflow.

STEP 11 - MUTUAL HANDSHAKE
--------------------------
When the required mutual acceptance is completed, the platform can move to the
contact-unlock stage.

STEP 12 - CONTACT UNLOCK
------------------------
Contact details can become available according to the platform's workflow and
permissions.

IMPORTANT:
This workflow describes the platform concept. Actual status, availability,
matches, quotations and handshake state must always come from the backend record.
